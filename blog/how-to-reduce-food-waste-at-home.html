How to Reduce Food Waste at Home

Simple habits to track expiry dates, organize your fridge, and save money every week.

1. Track expiry dates

Know the difference between "use by" (safety) and "best before" (quality). Set reminders so nothing gets forgotten.

2. Organize by zone

Group items in your fridge, freezer, and pantry. Use first-in-first-out rotation: newer items go to the back.

3. Plan before you shop

Check what you already have before buying more. Apps like FreshGuard help you see your inventory at a glance.

4. Cook what you have

Use recipes that match ingredients already in stock instead of buying new ones for every meal.

5. Measure your savings

Track consumed, donated, and discarded items. Small changes add up to real money saved.
