About SmartLife Innovations

Independent editorial platform for practical mobile apps and everyday technology.

SmartLife Innovations is an independent editorial platform around mobile apps, technology and digital solutions.

We build practical tools to reduce everyday waste — starting with FreshGuard, an anti-food-waste inventory app with expiry reminders, barcode scanner, recipes and savings stats.

Our Mission

Help people live more sustainably through simple, privacy-respecting technology. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking — your data stays on your device.

The Editor

SmartLife Innovations is published by an independent developer passionate about reducing food waste and digital clutter. Based on the belief that technology should serve people, not exploit them.

Our Values

  • Privacy-first: data stays on your device
  • No fake expiry dates: you control every date
  • Colour never the only signal: text labels always accompany status
  • Offline-first: core features work without internet
  • No dark patterns: clear, honest user interfaces
